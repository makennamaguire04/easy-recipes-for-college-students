Quick, Easy Recipes for College Students

Busy day? These ten easy meals use familiar ingredients and simple instructions. Whether you're in a dorm, apartment, or shared kitchen, you can make something satisfying without spending your whole evening cooking.

Recipe 01

Cheesy Black Bean Quesadillas

15 minutes · 2 servings

Crispy tortillas, melted cheese, and seasoned beans make a filling lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 large tortillas
  • 1 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1/2 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 teaspoon taco seasoning
  • 1 teaspoon oil
  • Salsa for serving

Instructions

  1. Mash half the beans and mix with the remaining beans, pepper, and seasoning.
  2. Add the filling and cheese to each tortilla and fold in half.
  3. Cook in a lightly oiled skillet for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and melted.
  4. Serve with salsa.
Student tip: Use frozen peppers or leftover vegetables to save time.
Recipe 02

Peanut Butter Noodles

15 minutes · 2 servings

A creamy, savory noodle bowl that is easy to customize.

Ingredients

  • 6 ounces noodles
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon lime juice or vinegar
  • 2–4 tablespoons warm water
  • 1 cup shredded carrots

Instructions

  1. Cook noodles according to package directions and drain.
  2. Whisk peanut butter, soy sauce, honey, lime juice, and warm water until smooth.
  3. Toss noodles with sauce, adding water if needed.
  4. Top with shredded carrots.
Student tip: Add cooked edamame or frozen broccoli for extra nutrition.
Recipe 03

Easy Egg Fried Rice

15 minutes · 2 servings

A great way to turn leftover rice into a quick dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked, chilled rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 green onion, optional

Instructions

  1. Scramble the eggs in a skillet until fully cooked, then set aside.
  2. Heat the oil and cook the vegetables until hot.
  3. Add rice and soy sauce, breaking up clumps and stir-frying until steaming hot.
  4. Mix the eggs back in and garnish with green onion.
Student tip: Refrigerate cooked rice promptly and reheat leftovers until steaming hot.
Recipe 04

Tomato Spinach Pasta

20 minutes · 2 servings

A simple pasta dinner with a quick tomato sauce and greens.

Ingredients

  • 6 ounces pasta
  • 1 cup canned crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup spinach, fresh or frozen
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • Grated cheese, optional

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to package directions.
  2. Warm oil in a pan and cook garlic briefly.
  3. Add tomatoes and Italian seasoning; simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in spinach until hot, then toss with drained pasta.
  5. Finish with grated cheese if desired.
Student tip: Frozen spinach works well and helps reduce food waste.
Recipe 05

Loaded Microwave Sweet Potato

10–12 minutes · 1 serving

A filling meal made with a microwave and a few affordable toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium sweet potato
  • 1/2 cup black beans
  • 2 tablespoons salsa
  • 2 tablespoons plain yogurt
  • A pinch of cumin

Instructions

  1. Pierce the potato several times with a fork.
  2. Microwave until soft, turning halfway through cooking.
  3. Warm the beans separately.
  4. Split the potato and top with beans, salsa, yogurt, and cumin.
Student tip: Swap the sweet potato for a regular potato if it costs less.
Recipe 06

Chickpea Salad Sandwich

10 minutes · 2 servings

A no-fuss sandwich filling with chickpeas for plant-based protein.

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 tablespoons plain yogurt or mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • Chopped celery or cucumber
  • 4 slices bread or 2 rolls
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Mash chickpeas with a fork in a bowl.
  2. Mix in yogurt or mayonnaise, mustard, and chopped vegetables.
  3. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Spoon onto bread or rolls and serve with fruit or vegetables.
Student tip: Make the filling ahead and store it covered in the refrigerator.
Recipe 07

Cheesy Veggie Omelet

10 minutes · 1 serving

A quick breakfast that also works for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup chopped vegetables
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 teaspoon oil
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a skillet and cook vegetables until tender.
  2. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper and pour into the skillet.
  3. Cook until nearly set, then sprinkle cheese over one side.
  4. Fold the omelet and cook until the eggs are fully done.
Student tip: Use leftover vegetables so you don't have to buy ingredients just for breakfast.
Recipe 08

Tuna Lemon Rice Bowl

10 minutes · 1 serving

A pantry-friendly bowl that comes together in minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 1 can tuna, drained
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat rice and peas until hot.
  2. Flake tuna into the bowl.
  3. Add lemon juice, olive oil, and black pepper.
  4. Mix gently and serve warm.
Student tip: Frozen peas are easy to portion and work in lots of other meals.
Recipe 09

Crispy Bean Tacos

15 minutes · 2 servings

Crunchy tacos with creamy beans and fresh toppings.

Ingredients

  • 4 small tortillas
  • 1 cup refried beans
  • 1/2 cup shredded cabbage
  • Salsa
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 teaspoon oil

Instructions

  1. Spread beans over half of each tortilla.
  2. Add a little cheese and fold the tortillas closed.
  3. Toast in a lightly oiled skillet until crisp on both sides.
  4. Serve with cabbage and salsa.
Student tip: Cabbage often lasts longer in the fridge than delicate salad greens.
Recipe 10

Apple Cinnamon Oatmeal

8 minutes · 1 serving

A cozy breakfast made with inexpensive pantry staples.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1 cup milk or water
  • 1 small apple, chopped
  • Cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon peanut butter, optional

Instructions

  1. Cook oats with milk or water according to the package.
  2. Stir in chopped apple and cinnamon.
  3. Continue cooking until the oats are creamy and the apple softens.
  4. Finish with peanut butter if desired.
Student tip: Use whichever fruit is on sale, such as a banana or pear.

Keep cooking simple

Plan around ingredients you already have, refrigerate perishable leftovers promptly, and freeze portions you won't eat within 3–4 days.
