Five ways to stretch your grocery budget

  1. Plan meals around ingredients you already have.
  2. Compare unit prices and store brands.
  3. Choose frozen vegetables when they offer better value.
  4. Reuse ingredients across several meals.
  5. Freeze extra portions before they spoil.
