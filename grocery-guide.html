The student shopping playbook

How to grocery shop on a college budget

  • Plan three to five meals: Choose recipes before going to the store so you buy what you need.
  • Reuse ingredients: Buy spinach for pasta, eggs for breakfast, and rice for bowls and stir-fries.
  • Compare unit prices: Check the price per ounce or pound instead of looking only at the package price.
  • Choose flexible staples: Oats, rice, pasta, lentils, beans, and frozen vegetables can work across many recipes.
  • Shop with roommates: Split larger packages of ingredients when it makes sense for everyone.
  • Reduce food waste: Freeze extra portions and use fresh ingredients before they spoil.
