Student grocery-shopping tips

  • Plan a few meals before shopping and make a list of ingredients you need.
  • Compare unit prices when choosing between package sizes or brands.
  • Choose ingredients that work across multiple meals, such as rice, eggs, beans, frozen vegetables, and pasta.
  • Check store websites for current hours, weekly deals, and product availability.
