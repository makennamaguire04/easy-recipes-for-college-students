Three ways to shop smarter

01. Make a list

Plan a few meals before shopping so you buy what you need and avoid spending money on ingredients you won't use.

02. Compare prices

Check unit prices, store brands, and weekly deals. Frozen produce can also be a practical option.

03. Shop with a plan

Choose ingredients that work in multiple meals, such as rice, beans, eggs, pasta, and vegetables.
