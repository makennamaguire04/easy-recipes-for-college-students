Easy meals for college students to meal prep

Make busy weekdays easier with a little planning. These flexible recipes help you prepare breakfast, lunch, or dinner ahead of time without spending your entire Sunday in the kitchen.

The goal: Cook a few basics, mix and match ingredients, and give yourself more time for everything else.

Start with a simple system

Your 60-minute meal prep routine

01. Choose

Pick two meals and one breakfast for the week. Check your fridge and pantry before shopping.

02. Cook

Prepare rice, roast vegetables, cook your protein, and mix a sauce while different items cook.

03. Portion

Divide meals into containers, label them with the date, and refrigerate or freeze promptly.

Recipe 01 · Lunch or dinner

Build-Your-Own Burrito Bowls

30 minutes · 4 servings

One batch creates four colorful bowls. Change up the toppings each day to keep lunch interesting.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups uncooked rice, prepared according to the package
  • 2 cans black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups frozen corn
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 2 teaspoons taco seasoning
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • Salsa and shredded cheese, to serve

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice according to the package directions.
  2. Heat oil in a skillet. Add the bell pepper and corn and cook until tender and heated through.
  3. Stir in the black beans and taco seasoning. Add a small splash of water and cook until hot.
  4. Divide the rice and bean mixture among four containers. Pack salsa and cheese separately if possible.
  5. Finish with lime juice when serving. Add fresh lettuce or avocado on the day you eat the bowl.
Meal prep tip: Refrigerate only the portions you plan to eat within 3–4 days. Freeze later portions promptly and thaw safely in the refrigerator.
Recipe 02 · Breakfast

Peanut Butter Banana Overnight Oats

5 minutes prep · 1 serving

A grab-and-go breakfast you can mix the night before. Multiply the ingredients to prepare several jars at once.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup milk or unsweetened soy milk
  • 1/4 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter
  • 1/2 banana, sliced
  • A pinch of cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Combine the oats, milk, yogurt, peanut butter, and cinnamon in a jar or lidded container.
  2. Stir well, cover, and refrigerate overnight or for at least four hours.
  3. Stir again in the morning. Add the banana just before eating.
  4. Add a splash of milk if you prefer a thinner texture.
Meal prep tip: Keep the banana separate until breakfast to help it stay fresh. Eat refrigerated overnight oats within 3–4 days.
Recipe 03 · Lunch or dinner

Roasted Veggie & Chickpea Bowls

35 minutes · 3 servings

A colorful, plant-based meal with filling chickpeas, roasted vegetables, and a simple lemon dressing.

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups chopped broccoli or cauliflower
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 cups cooked rice or quinoa
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 lemon
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Pat the chickpeas dry and chop the vegetables into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Toss the chickpeas and vegetables with olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread on a baking sheet and roast for 20–25 minutes, turning halfway through, until the vegetables are tender.
  4. Serve over cooked rice or quinoa. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over each bowl.
  5. Divide into containers after cooking and refrigerate or freeze the portions you will not eat soon.
Meal prep tip: Store dressing separately and add it when serving. Swap in carrots, zucchini, or frozen vegetables depending on what's affordable.
Your Sunday checklist

Set up your week in advance

  • Check what you already have before writing your grocery list.
  • Choose recipes that reuse rice, beans, vegetables, and sauces.
  • Wash produce and portion snacks after shopping.
  • Label containers with the meal name and preparation date.
  • Refrigerate perishable food within two hours; freeze meals you won't eat within 3–4 days.
