Easy Meals for College Students to Meal Prep

Spend less time cooking during the week with make-ahead breakfasts, lunch bowls, and easy dinners. These recipes work for shared apartments, busy schedules, and anyone who wants to make groceries go further.

Recipe 01

Build-Your-Own Burrito Bowls

30 minutes · 4 servings

A flexible lunch or dinner with rice, beans, and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 cans black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups corn
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 2 teaspoons taco seasoning
  • Salsa and shredded cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook rice according to package directions.
  2. Sauté the bell pepper and corn until tender.
  3. Add black beans and taco seasoning, plus a splash of water.
  4. Divide rice and bean mixture among four containers.
  5. Store salsa and cheese separately and add when serving.
Prep tip: Refrigerate portions you will eat within 3–4 days and freeze later portions promptly.
Recipe 02

Peanut Butter Banana Overnight Oats

5 minutes prep · 1 serving

A grab-and-go breakfast you can prepare the night before.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter
  • 1/2 banana
  • Cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Mix oats, milk, yogurt, peanut butter, and cinnamon in a jar.
  2. Cover and refrigerate overnight or at least four hours.
  3. Stir in the morning and add sliced banana before eating.
  4. Add a splash of milk if you prefer a thinner texture.
Prep tip: Prepare several jars, keeping fruit separate until serving.
Recipe 03

Roasted Veggie Chickpea Bowls

35 minutes · 3 servings

A colorful, plant-based meal with chickpeas and roasted vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 3 cups chopped vegetables
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Garlic powder
  • 2 cups cooked rice or quinoa
  • 1 lemon

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Toss chickpeas and vegetables with oil and garlic powder.
  3. Roast for 20–25 minutes, turning halfway through.
  4. Divide among containers with cooked grains.
  5. Add lemon juice when serving.
Prep tip: Swap vegetables based on sales and what is already in your refrigerator.
Recipe 04

Pesto Pasta Lunch Boxes

20 minutes · 3 servings

A simple pasta lunch that tastes good chilled or reheated.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces pasta
  • 3 tablespoons pesto
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup chickpeas
  • 1 cup spinach

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to the package and drain.
  2. Let it cool slightly, then toss with pesto.
  3. Add halved tomatoes, chickpeas, and spinach.
  4. Divide into containers and refrigerate promptly.
Prep tip: Add a splash of water or a little extra pesto before serving if the pasta seems dry.
Recipe 05

Sheet-Pan Chicken and Vegetables

35 minutes · 4 servings

A balanced meal with easy cleanup and plenty of room for ingredient swaps.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound chicken breast, cubed
  • 4 cups chopped vegetables
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Paprika, salt, and pepper
  • 2 cups cooked rice

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet.
  2. Toss chicken and vegetables with oil and seasonings.
  3. Spread on the sheet and roast until vegetables are tender and chicken reaches 165°F internally.
  4. Serve with rice and divide into four containers.
Prep tip: Use broccoli, carrots, or whatever vegetables are affordable that week.
Recipe 06

Freezer-Friendly Bean Chili

30 minutes · 4 servings

A hearty pot of chili that can become several easy meals.

Ingredients

  • 1 can kidney beans
  • 1 can black beans
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup corn
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • Chili powder and cumin
  • 1 cup water or broth

Instructions

  1. Sauté onion in a pot until softened.
  2. Add beans, tomatoes, corn, spices, and water or broth.
  3. Bring to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Cool promptly and divide into individual containers.
  5. Refrigerate near-term portions and freeze the rest.
Prep tip: Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator and reheat until steaming hot.
Recipe 07

Egg and Veggie Breakfast Muffins

25 minutes · 6 muffins

Portable egg bites for mornings when you need breakfast quickly.

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 1 cup chopped spinach or peppers
  • 1/3 cup shredded cheese
  • Salt and pepper
  • Oil for greasing the pan

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 350°F and grease six muffin cups.
  2. Beat eggs and mix with vegetables, cheese, salt, and pepper.
  3. Divide mixture between the cups.
  4. Bake for 18–22 minutes until the centers are set.
  5. Cool promptly and refrigerate or freeze in portions.
Prep tip: Reheat thoroughly before eating and use within safe storage times.
Recipe 08

Lentil Curry with Rice

35 minutes · 4 servings

A comforting, budget-friendly meal with pantry ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 can light coconut milk
  • 1 tablespoon curry powder
  • 1 cup water
  • Cooked rice for serving

Instructions

  1. Combine lentils, tomatoes, coconut milk, curry powder, and water in a pot.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Cook for 18–22 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lentils are tender.
  4. Add water if the curry becomes too thick.
  5. Divide with rice into meal containers.
Prep tip: Add frozen spinach near the end of cooking for extra vegetables.
Recipe 09

Greek-Inspired Couscous Bowls

20 minutes · 3 servings

A fresh lunch with chickpeas, vegetables, and a simple lemon dressing.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup couscous
  • 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1 cucumber, chopped
  • 1 cup tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Dried oregano

Instructions

  1. Prepare couscous according to package directions.
  2. Mix lemon juice, olive oil, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  3. Combine couscous with chickpeas and chopped vegetables.
  4. Toss with dressing and divide into containers.
Prep tip: Keep dressing separate if you prefer your vegetables extra crisp.
Recipe 10

Yogurt Berry Breakfast Jars

5 minutes prep · 3 servings

A quick breakfast with yogurt, fruit, and a crunchy topping.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups plain yogurt
  • 1 1/2 cups berries, fresh or frozen
  • 1 cup oats or granola
  • Cinnamon, optional

Instructions

  1. Divide yogurt between three containers.
  2. Add berries and a sprinkle of cinnamon.
  3. Cover and refrigerate.
  4. Add oats or granola just before eating so they stay crunchy.
Prep tip: Frozen berries can be economical and thaw in the refrigerator overnight.

Your weekly meal-prep checklist

  • Plan meals before grocery shopping.
  • Cook ingredients that can be used in more than one recipe.
  • Label containers with the preparation date.
  • Refrigerate perishable food within two hours.
  • Freeze portions you won't eat within 3–4 days.
